Community Code of Conduct

Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, child-centered community. Participation must support safety, dignity, accessibility, and responsible collaboration.

Expected behavior

* Put children’s safety and privacy first.
* Communicate respectfully, even during disagreement.
* Separate evidence, opinion, and speculation.
* Welcome parents, caregivers, educators, researchers, advocates, technologists, and community partners.
* Use age-appropriate, trauma-aware language.
* Report mistakes and safety concerns responsibly.
* Respect intellectual property and confidentiality boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks
* Sexualized language or content involving minors
* Doxxing or sharing identifying information
* Publishing allegations or case details about an identifiable person
* Encouraging dangerous, illegal, exploitative, or abusive behavior
* Security testing without authorization
* Manipulating, impersonating, or targeting children or vulnerable people
* Soliciting private contact with minors
* Repeated disruption, spam, or commercial promotion unrelated to the mission

Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect people, the project, or the community. Serious safety concerns may be referred to an appropriate authority when legally required or reasonably necessary.

Reporting

Do not report sensitive child-safety incidents publicly. Use a private, authorized reporting channel when one is listed in SECURITY.md. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
