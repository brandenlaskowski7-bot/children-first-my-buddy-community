Community Participation Terms

These terms apply to issues, discussions, pull requests, comments, resource submissions, partnership-interest submissions, and other public participation.

Participation is public

Assume everything submitted is visible, searchable, shareable, and permanent. Do not submit confidential information or anything you would not want publicly associated with you or your organization.

Your promises

By participating, you confirm that:

* You are legally permitted to submit the material
* Your submission is accurate to the best of your knowledge
* You have removed personal and sensitive information
* Your submission does not identify a child or expose a person to harm
* You are not making a confidential disclosure
* You will follow the Code of Conduct and repository policies
* You are not submitting material to create an undisclosed ownership, payment, partnership, or licensing claim

Moderation

Maintainers may edit titles, apply labels, move discussions, request evidence, redact sensitive information, hide content, lock threads, close submissions, restrict accounts, or preserve information needed for safety or enforcement.

No professional relationship

Participation does not create an attorney-client, therapist-client, doctor-patient, teacher-student, fiduciary, employment, partnership, agency, joint-venture, or other professional relationship.

No promise of payment or adoption

Ideas may be evaluated, discussed, combined, independently developed, declined, or left unused. Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, participation does not create a right to compensation, royalties, credit beyond ordinary attribution, ownership of the project, or involvement in future development.

Protected discussions

Formal partnerships, confidential disclosures, proprietary demonstrations, inventions, source code, patent material, commercial terms, and sensitive safety reports require an approved private channel and, where appropriate, a separate written agreement.
