Community Governance

Stewardship

The Children First / My Buddy project steward maintains final responsibility for the public repository’s scope, safety rules, priorities, and published materials.

Decision principles

Public decisions should prioritize:

1. Child safety
2. Privacy and dignity
3. Responsible adult involvement
4. Evidence and transparency
5. Accessibility and inclusion
6. Security and misuse resistance
7. Practical value for families and communities
8. Protection of proprietary and confidential systems

Community input

Community participants may propose ideas, research, documentation, resources, or pilot concepts through issues and discussions. Maintainers evaluate submissions for mission alignment, safety, feasibility, evidence, legal and ethical concerns, and intellectual-property boundaries.

Maintainer authority

Maintainers may:

* Accept, revise, defer, or decline proposals
* Establish labels, milestones, and discussion categories
* Remove unsafe or sensitive content
* Restrict participation when community rules are violated
* Keep implementation details private

Public discussion does not create a vote, ownership interest, partnership, agency relationship, employment relationship, or promise of implementation.

Conflicts of interest

Participants should disclose financial, organizational, research, or personal interests that could reasonably affect a recommendation.
