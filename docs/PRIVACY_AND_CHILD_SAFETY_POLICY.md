Privacy and Child Safety Policy

Core rule

No public contribution may identify a child or disclose sensitive information about a child, family, alleged offender, victim, witness, reporter, caregiver, educator, or private individual.

Prohibited information

Do not publish:

* Names, faces, voices, images, videos, addresses, precise locations, schools, schedules, contact details, usernames, or device identifiers
* Private messages, recordings, screenshots, account contents, or browsing history
* Medical, mental-health, disability, education, counseling, legal, court, child-welfare, or law-enforcement information
* Allegations, reports, accusations, case timelines, or evidence tied to an identifiable person
* Passwords, tokens, security questions, recovery codes, internal links, or system details
* Data that becomes identifying when combined with other public information

Research and statistics

Use aggregated, de-identified information from reliable public sources. Cite the source, publication date, population, age range, geography, and known limitations. Do not present correlation as causation.

Images and media

Do not upload images or recordings of minors. Publicly licensed educational media may be linked only when lawful, necessary, respectful, and consistent with child-safety principles.

Safety reports

This repository is not a reporting channel for emergencies, abuse allegations, exploitation, self-harm risk, missing children, or active threats. Use the appropriate local authority, emergency service, platform safety channel, or qualified professional.

Removal

Maintainers may remove or restrict content without advance notice when privacy or safety may be at risk. Removal from GitHub may not erase copies, notifications, caches, forks, or third-party archives; contributors must therefore avoid posting sensitive information in the first place.
