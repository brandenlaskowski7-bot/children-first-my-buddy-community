Public/Private Intellectual-Property Boundary

Public layer

The public repository may contain:

* Mission and values
* Community governance
* General product vision
* Public safety principles
* Non-confidential use cases
* Educational resources
* Public research references
* Accessibility feedback
* Community and partnership interest

Protected private layer

The Protected Intelligence and Memory Layer is outside the scope of this repository. Public materials may describe only the authorized capabilities, expected outputs, external integration requirements, and user-facing behavior of that layer.

The following remain private unless the owner expressly approves a specific disclosure:

* Internal architecture, component names, relationships, and processing stages
* Methods that transform inputs into outputs
* Memory evaluation, classification, retention, routing, retrieval, or decision logic
* Algorithms, scoring, weighting, thresholds, prompts, system instructions, and behavioral methods
* Internal interfaces, schemas, security controls, enforcement mechanisms, and deployment configuration
* Source code, credentials, models, datasets, internal documentation, and test evidence
* Patent-sensitive designs, claims, experiments, diagrams, benchmarks, proofs, inventions, and know-how
* Confidential partner, customer, legal, financial, research, or business information

Contribution rule

If you are uncertain whether information is safe for public release, do not post it. Describe the topic at a high level and request a private review channel.

Public discussion does not waive confidentiality, ownership, patent rights, trade-secret rights, copyrights, trademarks, contractual rights, or licensing restrictions. Contributors should treat the protected layer as a black box and must not request or attempt to reconstruct its internal operation.
