Public Roadmap

This roadmap covers public community work only. It does not disclose or commit to proprietary engineering milestones.

Phase 1 — Foundation

* Publish mission, scope, conduct, contribution, safety, and intellectual-property boundary documents
* Establish public issue templates
* Gather initial feedback from parents, caregivers, educators, advocates, and safety professionals
* Build a reviewed public resource index

Phase 2 — Community Requirements

* Document age-group needs and caregiver concerns
* Collect accessibility requirements
* Develop non-confidential user journeys
* Identify measurable child-safety and digital well-being outcomes
* Define public pilot-participant criteria

Phase 3 — Partnership Readiness

* Publish a non-confidential partnership pathway
* Identify research, education, nonprofit, community, and technology collaborators
* Create public pilot principles
* Establish private channels for protected disclosures and formal proposals

Phase 4 — Public Learning

* Share approved, non-confidential findings
* Publish safety and accessibility lessons
* Maintain a transparent public change log
* Expand community education and resources

Not represented here

The Protected Intelligence and Memory Layer, private product architecture, security controls, source code, patent strategy, internal methods, and confidential partner work are outside this public roadmap.
